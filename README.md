### 👋 About Me

I build mobile and AI products end to end, from the first brief to the store release, and I own features rather than tickets. Sole mobile engineer on **Corteksa**, a multi-tenant B2B CRM used by Saudi businesses, and builder of my own AI apps on Google Play.

- 🤖 **AI in production:** the app side of a multi-agent AI assistant (streamed agent and tool-call events, human approval before any data changes), on-device **Whisper** speech-to-text, **Gemini** structured extraction, and **Claude** / Gemini in my own tools
- 📱 **Mobile, native when it matters:** Flutter for the product, native Kotlin and Swift where Flutter can't reach, like replying from a notification with the app fully closed
- 🔌 **Real-time and integrations:** WebSocket messaging across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and TikTok, plus Gmail, Outlook and IMAP email
- 📴 **Offline-first:** a durable outbox with ordering, backoff, crash recovery and idempotency keys, so nothing is lost or sent twice
- 🔐 **Security:** encrypted token storage (Keychain, Android Keystore), single-flight token refresh, role-based permissions
- 🧪 **Quality:** 4,000+ unit, widget and BLoC tests, and one CI/CD pipeline that ships both platforms
- 🌍 **Built for the region:** Arabic (full RTL), English and Turkish as a first-class concern, not a retrofit
